Help with Scrolled, the iPhone app.

Contact us

For help, bug reports or ideas, email scrolledhelp@gmail.com.

The quickest way is send feedback in Scrolled's settings. It opens an email to us with your app version, iOS version and iPhone model filled in at the bottom, which helps with bug reports. You can edit or delete any of it before you send it.

Subscriptions

  • Cancel or change your plan in your iPhone's Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. Deleting Scrolled doesn't cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • New iPhone, or reinstalled Scrolled? Use “restore purchases” in the app while signed in with the same Apple ID.
