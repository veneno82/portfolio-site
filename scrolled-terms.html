8. Hard mode and uninstall protection

These settings are optional, and they only turn on when you choose them.

  • Hard mode makes turning blocking off slow on purpose. While it's on, you can add apps to your block list but not remove them, and turning blocking or hard mode off means typing a sentence and then waiting 24 hours. Blocking stays on while you wait.
  • Uninstall protection asks iOS to stop apps from being deleted, so Scrolled can't be deleted to skip the wait. It comes on with hard mode, and can also be turned on by itself. iOS applies this setting to the whole device, so while it's on, other apps may not be deletable either.
  • You turn these on yourself, and you accept these effects when you do. Scrolled has no servers, so we can't shorten the wait or switch these settings off for you. Before turning them on, make sure you won't need to delete apps or change your blocking for the next day or so.
